The light cycle

Red, then green, then amber, then back to red. After 3 steps it returns to the start, which is why mathematicians call it a cyclic group.

Show the group table

g means "go to the next light". g³ = e means three steps return to the start.

Fixed-time vs adaptive signals

One intersection, 600 seconds, the same random arrivals for both controllers. North–South is busier than East–West.

What the controllers do

Fixed: switches every 20 s regardless of queues.
Adaptive: holds green at least 6 s, then switches when the other side has the longer queue (or the current side is empty), and never holds beyond 30 s. Each switch costs 3 s of amber.

Average wait is total queue-time divided by vehicles that arrived (Little's law).
